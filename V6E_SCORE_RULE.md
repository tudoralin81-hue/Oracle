# Oracle Growth V6e — Score Adjustment

Canonical rule:

- Raw score 0–95: final score unchanged.
- Raw score 96: final score = 94 (−2).
- Raw score 97–100: final score = raw score − 3.

The raw score must remain available internally for diagnostics/regression. The adjustment is applied only to the final displayed/ranking score where the V6e scoring contract requires it.

This rule is intentionally non-overlapping at the boundaries.
