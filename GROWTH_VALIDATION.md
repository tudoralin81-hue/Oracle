# Growth validation

## 100% validation gate

Growth is considered FINAL only when every item below is true:

### Data independence
- Market data is obtained directly by Oracle's market-data layer; WordPress is not a source for Growth calculations.
- OHLCV is available for the evaluated ticker before technical indicators are calculated.
- Missing market data never becomes a fabricated zero or a false technical signal.

### Twelve-factor engine
- Every recommendation is generated from the same frozen 12-factor definition used by the Oracle Growth specification.
- SHORT, MEDIUM and LONG use their exact frozen weight profiles.
- Each profile totals exactly 100 points.
- No recommendation ticker, score, forecast or signal is hard-coded as the result of the ranking.

### Decision engine
- Score is calculated by the engine from the 12 factors and weights.
- Risk is calculated by the engine from the specified risk rules.
- Forecast is calculated by the engine from the specified forecast rules.
- BUY / WATCH / AVOID is generated by the specified thresholds.
- The global universe is ranked and the best valid candidate for each horizon is selected; the three horizon results are then deduplicated according to the specification.

### Time / history
- T0 is the persisted 16:00 Europe/Bucharest anchor.
- Forecast uses T0 as its reference point.
- Growth history is append-only and records the recommendation state at each run.

### UI acceptance
- one Growth hero only (shared shell banner)
- no `VEZI METODOLOGIA ȘI PONDERILE` card
- three active recommendation cards: SHORT / MEDIUM / LONG
- tickers remain prominent
- compact 12-weight grid, 2 rows × 6
- VEEV ADX renders `—` when unavailable
- LONG profile is corrected to 100 points
